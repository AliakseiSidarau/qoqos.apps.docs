CardsOnHand Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

CardsOnHand ("the App") is an Android application that allows users to store and manage loyalty cards in one place. The App provides features such as card organization, favorites, search, barcode management, customizable card backgrounds, and data import and export.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is stored locally, what information is used for analytics, and how third-party services may process information when you use the App.

1. Account and Personal Information

CardsOnHand does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use its core features.

The App does not collect users' names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personally identifiable information for analytics.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Information that users add to CardsOnHand is stored locally on their device to provide the App's functionality. This may include:

  • Card names and labels.
  • Barcode numbers and barcode formats.
  • Card appearance and background preferences.
  • Favorite status and display preferences.
  • Card images added by the user.
  • Application settings, such as theme and language.

This information is used to display and manage loyalty cards within the App. Card names, barcode numbers, and card images are not collected for analytics and are not sent to the analytics service as part of the App's analytics functionality.

CardsOnHand does not operate its own backend server to store users' loyalty card collections.

3. Analytics and App Usage

CardsOnHand uses Firebase Analytics, a third-party analytics service provided by Google, to understand general application usage and improve the App.

Analytics is used to measure application usage, including the number of cards stored in the App and general interactions with its features.

The App's analytics functionality does not send card names, barcode numbers, card images, or the contents of individual loyalty cards to Firebase Analytics.

CardsOnHand does not intentionally send personally identifiable information through its analytics events.

Firebase Analytics may automatically collect certain technical and usage information, depending on its configuration. This may include app interaction events, app instance identifiers, device and app information, and general usage metrics.

Such information is processed by Firebase Analytics in accordance with Google's applicable privacy policies and service terms.

4. Barcodes, Camera Access, and Images

CardsOnHand allows users to add barcode information to their cards. Depending on the feature used, a barcode can be entered manually, scanned using the device camera, or obtained from an image selected by the user.

Camera access, when requested, is used to support barcode scanning. Images selected by users are processed to provide the requested card-related functionality.

Card images and barcode information are stored locally for use within the App. They are not collected for analytics and are not included in analytics events.

5. Import and Export

CardsOnHand provides import and export functionality so users can back up their card information and restore it later.

Exported files may contain card information required to restore the user's cards. Users control where these files are saved and whether they are shared with other applications or people.

Users should keep exported backup files secure because the files may contain loyalty card information.

6. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

CardsOnHand uses Firebase Analytics to analyze general application usage.

The App does not sell users' personal information. It does not send card names, barcode numbers, or card images to Firebase Analytics as part of its analytics functionality.

The App does not operate its own server for collecting or storing users' loyalty card collections.

Information may be processed by other applications or services if users choose to share exported files or use operating system features that involve third-party applications.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Users can remove individual cards within the App. Locally stored card information can also generally be removed by clearing the App's data through Android settings or uninstalling the App.

Uninstalling the App does not necessarily delete backup files that were previously exported or analytics information already processed by third-party services.

Analytics data is subject to the retention settings configured for Firebase Analytics and Google's applicable policies.

8. Data Security

CardsOnHand stores card information locally on the user's device. Users should keep their devices secure and use the security features provided by Android.

Users should also take care when exporting, storing, or sharing backup files containing loyalty card information.

9. Children's Privacy

CardsOnHand is a general-purpose utility application and is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

The App does not require account registration or the submission of personal information to use its core features.

10. Your Choices

Users control the card information they enter into CardsOnHand and can remove cards from the App.

Users can choose whether to export, store, or share backup files. Android device settings can be used to manage app permissions. Disabling a permission may prevent the related feature from working.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, its functionality, or applicable requirements. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
