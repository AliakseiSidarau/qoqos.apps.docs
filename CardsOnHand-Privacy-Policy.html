CardsOnHand Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

CardsOnHand ("the App") is an Android application that allows users to store and manage loyalty cards in one place. The App provides features such as card organization, favorites, search, barcode management, customizable card backgrounds, and data import and export.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the App, how that information is stored, and how third-party services may be involved.

1. Information We Collect

CardsOnHand does not require users to create an account to use its core features.

The App allows users to enter or create information associated with their loyalty cards, including:

  • Card names and labels.
  • Barcode numbers and barcode formats.
  • Card background and appearance preferences.
  • Favorite status and card display preferences.
  • Card photos or other images selected by the user, where applicable.
  • Application preferences, such as the selected theme and language.

The information stored in a card depends on what the user chooses to enter or import. Users should avoid adding sensitive personal information that is not necessary for managing their loyalty cards.

2. Local Data Storage

Card information and application preferences are intended to be stored locally on the user's device so that the App can provide its core functionality.

CardsOnHand does not operate its own account system or backend service for storing users' loyalty card collections. The App's core card management functionality does not require users to upload their card collections to a CardsOnHand server.

Data stored locally may be lost if the user clears the App's data, uninstalls the App, or loses access to the device, unless a separate backup has been created.

3. Barcodes and Card Images

CardsOnHand allows users to add barcode information to their cards. Depending on the feature used, a barcode may be entered manually, scanned using the device camera, or obtained from an image selected by the user.

If camera access is requested for barcode scanning, the camera is used to support that feature. If the user selects an image, the App processes the selected image for the requested functionality.

The App may also allow users to add a photo to a loyalty card. Card images and barcode information are used to provide the App's card management features.

This policy does not imply that images or barcode information are automatically uploaded to a server. Any additional processing by an operating system component or third-party library depends on the implementation of the relevant feature.

4. Import and Export

CardsOnHand provides import and export functionality so users can back up their card information and restore it later.

When exporting cards, the resulting backup may contain card information and other data required to restore the cards. The user controls where the exported file is saved and whether it is shared with another application or person.

When importing a backup, the App processes the selected file to restore the supported card information.

Users should store exported backup files securely because anyone who obtains access to a backup may be able to read the information it contains, depending on the file format and its contents.

5. Analytics

CardsOnHand includes Firebase Analytics, a third-party analytics service provided by Google, to help understand application usage and improve the App.

Depending on the Firebase configuration, analytics may process information such as:

  • App launches and general usage events.
  • Interactions with application features, where analytics events are configured.
  • App instance identifiers and technical information associated with analytics.
  • Device, app version, and usage information collected automatically by the analytics service, where applicable.

The exact information processed depends on the analytics configuration, the events implemented in the App, and the settings available to the analytics service.

Firebase Analytics is a third-party service. Its processing of information is also subject to Google's applicable privacy terms and policies.

CardsOnHand's local card storage is separate from analytics. This Privacy Policy does not claim that analytics receives loyalty card names, barcode numbers, or card images; whether any such information could be included in analytics events depends on the actual event implementation.

6. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

CardsOnHand uses Firebase Analytics for application analytics. The service may process certain technical and usage information as described above.

The App does not provide its own server for collecting and maintaining users' loyalty card collections. Card information is not intentionally shared by the App with third parties as part of its core local card-management functionality.

Information may be processed by other applications or services when users choose to share exported files or use operating system features that involve another application.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Users can manage and remove their cards within the App. Card information stored locally can generally be removed by deleting the relevant cards, clearing the App's storage through Android settings, or uninstalling the App.

Deleting the App does not necessarily delete backup files that were previously exported or information already processed by third-party services.

Analytics data processed by Firebase is subject to the service's retention settings and applicable Google policies.

8. Data Security

CardsOnHand uses the Android device's application storage mechanisms to support local data storage. Users should keep their devices secure and use the device's available security features.

No method of electronic storage or data transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should take care when exporting, storing, or sharing backup files containing loyalty card information.

9. Children's Privacy

CardsOnHand is a general-purpose utility application and is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

The App does not require children to provide personal information through an account registration system. However, third-party analytics services may process technical or usage information as described in this policy.

10. Your Choices

You can control the information you enter into CardsOnHand and can remove cards from the App.

You can also choose whether to create, store, or share exported backup files.

Android provides system-level controls for app permissions. You can review or change the permissions granted to CardsOnHand through your device settings. Disabling a permission may prevent the related feature from working.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, its functionality, or applicable requirements. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
