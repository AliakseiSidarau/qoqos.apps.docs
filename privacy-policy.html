DueSave Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

DueSave ("the App") is a mobile application designed to help users track important deadlines such as returns, warranties, gift cards, coupons, subscriptions, and documents.

Information We Collect

DueSave does not require users to create an account and does not collect personal information through a server.

Information entered by the user, such as deadline titles, dates, amounts, currencies, and deadline types, is stored locally on the user's device.

Data Storage

DueSave uses local device storage to store the information required for the application to function. Your deadline data is not uploaded to or stored on our servers.

Notifications

DueSave may use Android notifications to remind users about upcoming deadlines. Notification permission is requested where required by the Android operating system.

Analytics and Tracking

DueSave does not use analytics services, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies to monitor users' activity.

Third-Party Services

DueSave does not share users' deadline information with third parties.

Data Deletion

Because deadline information is stored locally on the device, users can delete their information directly within the application or by removing the application from their device.

Children's Privacy

DueSave is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
